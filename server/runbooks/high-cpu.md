# High CPU Utilisation

**Service:** application-service  
**Severity:** Warning

## Symptoms
CPU remains above 90% for ten minutes.

## Approved action
Review traffic and recommend a capacity increase. Require approval before changing capacity.

## Escalation
Escalate if latency continues to rise after capacity is increased.
